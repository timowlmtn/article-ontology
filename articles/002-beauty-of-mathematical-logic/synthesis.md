# Evidence synthesis — Beauty of mathematical logic

**Status:** not ready. The initial source set is entirely candidate material.

## Tensions to investigate

- A well-formed formula is not yet a sentence, a sentence is not yet true in every structure, and a formal derivation is not automatically an explanation to a human reader.
- A proof may be beautiful yet rely on unspoken background knowledge, notation, or trusted results.
- Formal verification can improve confidence while making the visible proof longer or less legible.
- An AI system may find a valid route without supplying the conceptual compression a human values.
- A later “clean” proof may depend on the earlier long proof's machinery rather than replace it.

## What cannot yet be claimed

- That AI makes mathematical proofs uglier or more beautiful.
- That compactness is the right case or that its proof is inherently beautiful or opaque.
- That an accepted Lean proof establishes informal mathematical truth without qualifications.
- That AlphaProof or LeanDojo understands mathematics in a human sense.
- That all mathematicians use “beauty” to mean simplicity, brevity, generality, or explanation.

## Case-selection test

Keep compactness only if close reading yields an intelligible statement, two visible intermediate moves, and an honest way to show why syntax and semantics meet. Otherwise select a smaller result from Malitz's model-theory sequence.

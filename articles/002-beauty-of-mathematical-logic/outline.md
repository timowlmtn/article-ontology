This is a research map, not an approved outline. Rebuild after the candidate sources have been accepted and read with precise locators.

1. **A formula is not yet a claim about a world**
   - Establish the difference between writing a first-order expression and interpreting it in a structure.
   - Risk: making the opening a technical glossary rather than an encounter.

2. **From formal language to satisfaction and truth**
   - Distinguish formation, interpretation, satisfaction, truth in a structure, and derivation.
   - Evidence burden: SRC-041, with one exact worked example.

3. **The compactness problem**
   - Test the distinctions through the relation between every finite portion of a theory and the whole theory.
   - Evidence burden: SRC-041's theorem, proof path, and two verified intermediate moves.

4. **Where AI enters—and where it does not**
   - Separate learned proof search from the deterministic checker and from the people who formalize, interpret, and explain.
   - Evidence burden: SRC-043 through SRC-045.

5. **The first proof and its afterlife**
   - Test the writer's idea that elegance can emerge through explanation, compression, and transfer without devaluing complex correct work.
   - Evidence burden: an accepted source on mathematical beauty and its strongest objection.

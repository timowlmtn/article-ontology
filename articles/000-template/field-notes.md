## Raw observations

Record before researching or polishing. Distinguish what was perceived from what was inferred. For each important encounter, ask:

1. What is materially present?
2. What tools, conventions, people, and institutions mediated it?
3. Whose authorship or labor is visible, and whose disappears?
4. Does the work resemble, record, construct, symbolize, or generate its subject?
5. What must exist for this work to be this kind of work?

# Article evidence

Keep prospective claims separate from draft prose. Duplicate this block for each important claim.

## CLM-001 — Short label

**PROSPECTIVE CLAIM**  
What we may want to say.

**CLAIM TYPE**  
fact / definition / historical interpretation / causal claim / critical judgment / writer reflection / AI inference

**SUPPORT**  
- SRC-___ — paraphrase or short quotation, with page, section, or timestamp

**CHALLENGES**  
- SRC-___ — contradictory, limiting, or qualifying evidence

**WARRANT**  
Why this evidence supports this exact claim; identify any inferential step.

**SCOPE / QUALIFICATION**  
Where the claim applies, and what narrower wording the evidence requires.

**STATUS**  
`needs-research` / `hypothesis` / `weak` / `disputed` / `supported`

**NOTES**  
Writer's interpretation, caveats, and decisions still required.

## Evidence rules

- A citation is not support unless the source actually entails or documents the claim.
- Preserve the difference between what a source says and what the writer infers.
- Keep contradictory evidence in the same claim block rather than in a separate silo.
- Use `writer reflection` for experiential or critical judgments; do not disguise them as facts.
- AI output may suggest a claim or search path, but cannot occupy `SUPPORT`.

# Argumentative outline

Build this only after the key evidence is visible. For every section, state:

- what the section is trying to establish
- which claims and sources it relies on
- what objection or qualification it must address
- how it advances the overall argument

## Structural audit

- Does the article distinguish scene, source-grounded fact, interpretation, and argument?
- Does each section change the reader's understanding rather than repeat the thesis?
- Does the counterargument have enough space to matter?
- Is the conclusion earned by the evidence rather than announced in advance?

# Draft


Draft only after `synthesis.md` and `outline.md` expose the article's evidentiary logic. The writer owns voice, judgment, and conclusions.

During revision, temporary claim markers such as `[CLM-003]` may be useful. Remove them only after citations and the fact-check inventory preserve traceability.

## Revision questions

- Where does the prose move from sourced account to writer interpretation?
- Are important terms used consistently with the distinctions in the evidence?
- Does a vivid example genuinely support the argument, or merely decorate it?
- Has AI-generated phrasing flattened the writer's voice or overstated certainty?
- What would the most informed skeptical reader contest?

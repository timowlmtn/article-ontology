# Who is Bazan and why his ideas of Ontology are relevant when thinking about AI

As I thought about how knowledge and meaning relate to the real world, I 
found myself drawn to Ontologies, and ultimately, I discovered Bazan.

Bazan was a mid-nineteenth century film critic who published a series of
magazines that ultimately became one of the foundational texts for modern
film criticism.

So how does this relate to AI.  As it turns out, Bazan was tackling with 
a similiar that we see in our transitional where AI is a part of everything,
except that in his world, art was transitioning to the photographic and
moving image.

As a keen thinker, he sought to give philosophical rigor to this transition
and came up with the notion of Ontology, or the study of what something truly is.

In Bazan's case, he pondering who is the artist in the making of the film.
In an extreme case, for example a documentary, do we really think of the 
artist as the one holds the camera and records?  Then doesn't that make 
anyone who can hold a camera and record an event an artist?

The idea has a deeper question underlying and delves into the event
being captured and what is real about it, or what is the essense of it.

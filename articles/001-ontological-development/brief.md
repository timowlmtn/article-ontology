# Article brief

- **Working title:** Ontological Development
- **Central question:** How to use Ontology to Drive Effective AI Development
- **Why this matters:** Ontologies help us organize meaning and can provide the human/AI framework for meaningful agent-based development.
- **Intended reader:** Developers and AI practitioners
- **Tentative thesis:** My thesis is that Ontologies allow us to create file structures that lend well to the human side of AI development.
- **Scope:** This is a 500 word article for a newsletter.
- **Out of scope:** Actual coding or consulting advice.
- **Writer's existing beliefs:** My belief is that Ontologies will make developers more effective and reduce AI costs.
- **What could change my mind:** If Ontologies are not related to AI and are too complex.
- **Current stage:** Setting up.

My thesis is based on two experiences: One, I created a database migration assessment leveraging AI, and
I used folder structures to manage the artifacts I created during that assessment, and two, a database 
remediation consulting project where I was analyzing an existing system and providing recommendations.

What I'm looking for writing a guide for developers to understand what ontologies are, and how they can help
you to organize your interactions with AI agents.

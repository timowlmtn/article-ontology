# Brief — Benjamin, technological reproducibility, and AI authorship

- **Working topic:** Benjamin's “Work of Art” essay as a framework for technological mediation and AI authorship
- **Bounded question:** Which changes in authenticity, agency, reception, and politics produced by technological reproducibility help locate the auteur in AI-assisted work?
- **Why several articles may need it:** The framework applies to AI images, synthetic film, platform circulation, creative labor, copies, provenance, and mass reception.
- **In scope:** SRC-040; version history needed for citation; aura; authenticity; cult/exhibition value; apparatus; film performance and editing; reception; politics; one documented AI-work comparison.
- **Out of scope:** A complete account of Benjamin's philosophy; a general history of Frankfurt School theory; a verdict on copyright law; claims that Benjamin predicted AI.
- **Competing accounts expected:** loss-of-aura reading; emancipatory technology reading; political mass-media reading; accounts in which aura persists, returns, or is manufactured.
- **Completion condition:** A page-grounded account can state what changes under reproducibility, apply it to one AI workflow, and identify the limits of the analogy.
- **Status:** framing; primary source cataloged as candidate, not yet accepted or close-read

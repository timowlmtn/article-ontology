# Deep dive 002 — Bazin, representation, and realism

## Purpose

Understand Bazin as both theorist and working critic. Separate photographic genesis, resemblance, realism, film form, spectator freedom, ambiguity, and truth before asking what survives in digital or synthetic images.

## Bounded question

How does Bazin move from claims about what photography is to judgments about what cinema can do, and where do later scholars challenge that movement?

## Why reuse this

The resulting map can support articles on photography, documentary, realism, montage, digital cinema, and criticism—not only generative AI.

## Two-session reading plan

### Session A — Bazin's own movement

1. Read SRC-001 and tag passages `GENESIS`, `RESEMBLANCE`, `PSYCHOLOGY`, `TIME`, `BELIEF`, or `VALUE`.
2. Read one film-language essay from SRC-002.
3. Choose one Bazin review and watch the relevant film or scene.
4. Write what the concrete criticism adds, changes, or contradicts.

### Session B — The modern dispute

1. Read one chapter from SRC-005 and the full text of SRC-007 or SRC-008 if accessible.
2. Read SRC-012 or the relevant section of SRC-013.
3. Construct the strongest indexical reading and the strongest anti-reductionist reading.
4. Do not decide between them until both have locators.

# Brief — Video generation and world models

## Research objective

Explain LeCun's representation-space approach and assess whether present video generators answer his criticism of pixel-level prediction.

## Included

- LeCun's papers, talks, and research program
- V-JEPA and V-JEPA 2
- first-party generative-video reports
- independent evaluations of physics, state persistence, and temporal coherence
- the bounded relevance of those findings to Bazinian realism

## Excluded unless the article later needs them

- a product-by-product buyer's guide
- copyright, labor, or environmental impact
- claims about sentience or general intelligence
- a verdict on whether AI-generated cinema is art

## Completion condition

A source-grounded comparison can distinguish image plausibility, physical consistency, causal prediction, planning, narrative continuity, and cinematic realism.

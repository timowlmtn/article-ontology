# Synthesis — Video generation and world models

**Status:** not ready. Complete source acceptance and close reading first.

## Working distinctions

| Term | Working meaning | Does not by itself establish |
|---|---|---|
| Visual plausibility | Frames look locally convincing | correct causality or stable state |
| Physical consistency | Motion and interaction obey selected constraints | narrative or artistic truth |
| World model | predicts how a represented state changes, potentially under action | rendered video |
| Temporal persistence | identities, objects, and consequences remain coherent | significant duration or meaning |
| Cinematic realism | historically and critically contested relation among world, form, spectator, and truth | literal naturalism |

## Competing technical hypotheses

- **LeCun:** useful prediction should occur in a learned abstract representation that can discard unpredictable detail.
- **Generative-simulation thesis:** scaling generative video can produce increasingly capable simulators, with world-relevant properties emerging from the generation objective.

The current evidence does not close the dispute. It does show why a beautiful clip is not sufficient evidence for understanding, and why a benchmark failure is not sufficient evidence against art.

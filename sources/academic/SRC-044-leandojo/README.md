## Why it matters

LeanDojo provides a reproducible case in which language-model-based proof search retrieves premises and interacts programmatically with Lean. It can ground a precise account of learned premise selection and search, while Lean's environment supplies checkable feedback.

## Reading guidance

Separate benchmark performance from a claim of mathematical understanding. Record the formal corpus, task, accessible-premise rules, evaluation split, and the system's own stated limitations.

# Reference deck — Ontology, Bazin, representation, and AI

This is the writer-facing route through the source library. It is designed to help find sources quickly, assemble a bounded reading corpus, and decide what earns a place in an article.

- **Related article:** [001-bazin-and-ai](../../articles/001-bazan-and-ai/README.md)
- **Deck tags:** `topic/ontology` `topic/film` `topic/ai` `topic/representation`
- **Status:** assembling

The deck does not imply acceptance. Record the writer's decision in `research/source-catalog.md`; record local preservation in `sources/index.md`; record reading in `notes/literature/`.

## How to use the deck

For each working session:

1. Choose one **anchor** source and at most two **companion** sources.
2. Open the original from the link below.
3. Record page, section, or timestamp locators while reading.
4. Write one sentence each for `SOURCE CLAIM`, `CHALLENGE`, and `WRITER IDEA`.
5. Decide `accept`, `reject`, or `defer` in the catalog.
6. Copy only accepted, article-relevant sources into the article's `sources.md`.

Avoid reading everything before thinking. The purpose of the deck is selection and comparison, not completion for its own sake.

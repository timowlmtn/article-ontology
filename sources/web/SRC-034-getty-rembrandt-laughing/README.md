## What the Getty documents

- **Work:** *Rembrandt Laughing*, about 1628, Rembrandt Harmenszoon van Rijn (Dutch, 1606–1669)
- **Medium and dimensions:** oil on copper; 22.2 × 17.1 cm (8 3/4 × 6 5/8 in.)
- **Description:** Getty identifies the work as an early self-portrait of Rembrandt, approximately 22 years old, shown in a soldier's costume with a steel gorget. The page describes short, expressive brushstrokes and records the monogram “RHL.”
- **Attribution context:** Getty reports that scholarly analysis and scientific testing after the work re-entered public view supported its attribution to Rembrandt.

## Citation guidance and limits

- Cite for the museum's object metadata, Getty's attribution context, and its interpretation of the work as an early self-portrait/tronie.

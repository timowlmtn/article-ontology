## Why this record exists

This is an accessible institutional introduction to the camera obscura. It explains how a darkened chamber and lens project an external scene, notes that the projected image must be traced in reverse, and situates the device as a predecessor to photography. It is useful for a brief explanatory reference and for giving non-specialist readers a readable route into optical history.

## Boundaries and citation guidance

- Use for the device's basic operation and as an orientation source.
- Do not use it alone for contested claims about specific artists' use of optical devices, or for detailed historical chronology; seek specialist or primary sources for those claims.
- The page includes an embedded video; verify its wording directly before quoting it.

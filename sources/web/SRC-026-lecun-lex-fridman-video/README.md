## Why this record exists

This is the source for the widely circulated LeCun comparison between present language models and animal intelligence. It is useful for distinguishing the commonly paraphrased claim that AI “will never be smarter than a cat” from what he actually says: systems based on current language-model approaches will not reach even the level of a cat’s or dog’s understanding of the world.

## Scope

The article gives a popular-history account of U-locks/D-locks, from the 1972 patent associated with Stan Kaplan through later locking, material, and anti-grinder developments. It may orient research into the lock visible in *Bicycle Thieves* and into the material culture implied by bicycle-theft scenes.

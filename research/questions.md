# Series research questions

These questions form a tentative progression. They are not article assignments or assumed theses.

| ID | Question | Status | Why it comes here |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q-001 | What does “ontology” mean across philosophy, information science, knowledge representation, and contemporary AI? | not started | Establishes the term before applying it |
| Q-002 | How do ontologies differ from taxonomies, schemas, knowledge graphs, embeddings, vector databases, metadata, and document collections? | not started | Clarifies neighboring concepts and disputed boundaries |
| Q-003 | What kinds of context problems do current AI systems actually have? | not started | Identifies the problem before proposing ontology as a remedy |
| Q-004 | What can explicit semantic relationships add to retrieval-augmented generation, and what can they not add? | not started | Tests the ontology–RAG connection without presuming benefit |
| Q-005 | Can ontology improve context selection, memory, provenance, or agent reliability enough to justify its cost? | not started | Moves from mechanism to measurable value and tradeoffs |
| Q-006 | What does NotebookLM/Gemini Notebook teach about source-grounded research, and which lessons transfer to a local agent workflow? | not started | Studies a concrete reference model without treating it as a blueprint |
| Q-007 | Where should ontology live in a practical AI architecture, and how would an ontology-centered project be built and evaluated? | not started | Connects research to architecture and experiments |
| Q-008 | When is ontology unnecessary overhead or the wrong tool? | not started | Forces boundary conditions and counterexamples |

Suggested statuses: `not started`, `planning`, `discovering`, `reading`, `synthesizing`, `blocked`, `answered provisionally`.

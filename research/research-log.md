# Research log

Record consequential research activity, not every minor edit.

## Entry template

### YYYY-MM-DD — Short description

- **Question(s):** Q-___
- **Action:** What was searched, read, compared, or tested?
- **Result:** What changed in our understanding?
- **Sources affected:** SRC-___
- **Decisions for writer:** What needs human judgment?
- **Next step:** Smallest useful follow-up

## AI research record

When AI materially shapes a search or synthesis, also record:

- **AI role:** scout / reader / cartographer / adversary / interviewer / archivist / editor / fact-checker
- **Inputs provided:** file paths, source IDs, question IDs
- **Output disposition:** retained lead / verified and incorporated / rejected / unresolved
- **Human decision:** acceptance, thesis change, or scope decision remains the writer's

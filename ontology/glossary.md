# Glossary

Definitions here are starting questions, not settled meanings. Add source IDs as research develops.

## Ontology

- **Status:** provisional
- **Project navigation usage:** An explicit, revisable account of concepts in a domain and the relationships or constraints that hold among them.
- **Philosophical usage to investigate:** Inquiry into being or what exists, including the categories and modes of existence.
- **Aesthetic or medium usage to investigate:** Inquiry into what kind of thing an artwork or medium is, how it comes into being, and what relation it bears to a world.
- **Computational usage to investigate:** A formal or explicit representation of entities, categories, relationships, and sometimes axioms within a domain.
- **Do not collapse into:** taxonomy, database schema, or knowledge graph without examining the relevant usage.
- **Cross-disciplinary warning:** Shared vocabulary does not by itself establish a shared concept or causal connection.

## Photographic ontology

- **Status:** provisional; article-specific investigation underway
- **Working description:** Questions about what kind of object a photograph is and how its mode of production relates it to what was photographed.
- **Do not collapse into:** visual realism, stylistic naturalism, or the claim that cameras eliminate human agency.

## Auteur

- **Status:** provisional
- **Working description:** A critical attribution of organizing authorship, commonly to a director, inferred through control, recurring style, or meaning across works.
- **Open questions:** Is auteurism a theory, method, polemical policy, evaluative hierarchy, or some combination? How does it account for collaborative and computational production?

## Provenance

- **Status:** provisional
- **Working description:** A trace of origin, custody, transformation, and mediation connecting an assertion or artifact to its sources and production history.
- **Open question:** When photographic causation is absent, what kinds of provenance can warrant belief in or accountability for a synthetic image?

## Taxonomy

- **Status:** provisional
- **Working description:** A classification structure, often hierarchical.
- **Open question:** When does a taxonomy become part of, or get described as, an ontology?

## Knowledge graph

- **Status:** provisional
- **Working description:** A graph representation of entities and relationships, sometimes governed by an ontology.
- **Open question:** Which definitions require an explicit schema or ontology?

## Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)

- **Status:** provisional
- **Working description:** A family of architectures that retrieve external information to provide context for generation.
- **Open question:** What exactly is retrieved, selected, structured, and cited in different RAG systems?

## Embedding

- **Status:** provisional
- **Working description:** A learned numerical representation used to encode features or similarity.
- **Open question:** What semantic information is implicit in embeddings but explicit in ontologies?

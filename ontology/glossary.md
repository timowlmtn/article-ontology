# Glossary

Definitions here are starting questions, not settled meanings. Add source IDs as research develops.

## Ontology

- **Status:** provisional
- **Working description:** An explicit account of concepts in a domain and the relationships or constraints that hold among them.
- **Competing uses to investigate:** philosophical ontology; formal ontology; applied ontology in information science; ontology as a semantic artifact in AI systems.
- **Do not collapse into:** taxonomy, database schema, or knowledge graph without examining the relevant usage.

## Taxonomy

- **Status:** provisional
- **Working description:** A classification structure, often hierarchical.
- **Open question:** When does a taxonomy become part of, or get described as, an ontology?

## Knowledge graph

- **Status:** provisional
- **Working description:** A graph representation of entities and relationships, sometimes governed by an ontology.
- **Open question:** Which definitions require an explicit schema or ontology?

## Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)

- **Status:** provisional
- **Working description:** A family of architectures that retrieve external information to provide context for generation.
- **Open question:** What exactly is retrieved, selected, structured, and cited in different RAG systems?

## Embedding

- **Status:** provisional
- **Working description:** A learned numerical representation used to encode features or similarity.
- **Open question:** What semantic information is implicit in embeddings but explicit in ontologies?
